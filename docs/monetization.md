# Monetization Plan

## Positioning
Sell the product first as a service-assisted tool, then as repeatable software.

## Revenue lines
1. initial setup
2. monthly assistance
3. premium template packs
4. custom workflows

## Example pricing
- Starter setup: 149 EUR
- Pro setup: 299 EUR
- Monthly support: 29 to 99 EUR

## First niches
- associations
- theatre operators
- freelancers
- consultants
- small local businesses

## First sales logic
Do not wait for a perfect SaaS. Sell a pilot with real operational value.
